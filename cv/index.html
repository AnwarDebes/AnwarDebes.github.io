Research

CANDOR: interpretability by construction

Routes a layer through named concepts and emits a re-checkable certificate on every forward pass. On synthetic ground truth: δ ≈ 0.002 with no measurable accuracy cost; backdoor detection at AUC ≈ 0.90.

TRACE: a guardrail for AI agents

Readable content clauses, bounded-memory temporal monitors and a tamper-evident certificate ledger, on a CPU. F1 0.827 on deepset/prompt-injections at 107 µs per check, in 12.5 KiB.

VERITAS: formally verified guardrails

Safety properties proved or refuted by SAT and SMT solvers, with bypasses repaired by construction. Retraining stalls at 8 provable bypasses; constructive repair reaches 0, and accuracy goes from 0.69 to 0.71.

LUCID and graphtm-cbr

A glass-box, safety-shielded agent for ARC-AGI-3 that completes two public levels; and a graph Tsetlin Machine for Ames mutagenicity whose minimal-edit recourse succeeds for 95.5% of positives.

NEXUS: reinforcement learning for 2 to 6 players

AlphaZero-style Chinese Checkers agent with a 7.5M-parameter network and Gumbel MCTS, plus prototypes for coalition-aware search, decomposed values and a wreath-equivariant network. 324 tests pass on every push.

PocketOS and SleepArchitect, both live

A desktop environment that runs offline in a phone’s browser; and a sleep forecaster built on Borbély’s two-process model that reproduces all 11 published findings it was checked against, in 18.7 KB.
