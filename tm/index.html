What it learned, in plain logic

What you are looking at

Inputs x1 to x6 are random bits. The label is x1 XOR x2. Inputs x3 to x6 are pure noise, and a share of the training labels is flipped on purpose. Each bar is one Tsetlin automaton deciding whether one input, or its negation, belongs in one clause. Bars above the glass plane are included. Clauses marked + vote for class 1, clauses marked − vote for class 0, and the majority wins.

No GPUs were harmed. This is a real Tsetlin Machine running on your CPU in plain JavaScript. Research by @AnwarDebes · Research Universe
